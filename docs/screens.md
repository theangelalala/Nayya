# Screens

Every screen in the app. Add a row whenever you build one (the `build-screen` skill does this).
Live at `nayya-seven.vercel.app` + the path.

| Path | Screen | Surface | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | Titan foundations | (reference page) | Colors, type, spacing, breakpoints |
| `/components` | Component catalog | (Storybook) | Every component and its states |
| `/chat` | Home chat | Default Chat | Greeting, starter prompts, reminders |
| `/wizard` | Open Enrollment: who will be covered | Content Wizard | Exit opens the confirm modal |
| `/onboarding` | Account setup: a few more details | Onboarding | Name, birth date, address |
| `/settings` | Settings | Single Screen | Notification toggles |
| `/leave` | Leave planning: when is your baby due | Content Wizard | Date check, birth type, leave summary in chat |
| `/family` | Benefits setup: tell us about your family | Content Wizard | Relationship status, you, family members (name, relationship, birth date → age) |
